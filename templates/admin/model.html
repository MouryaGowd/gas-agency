What drives the prediction

How it works

For each customer, the model looks at the gaps between their past deliveries (latest, average, recent-weighted, variability), typical quantity, customer type and time of year, then predicts the gap until the next refill. Predicted restock date = last delivery + predicted gap.

Honest evaluation: the model is trained on the earlier 80% of orders and scored on the later 20% it never saw. The likely window comes from the 10th–90th percentile of those errors ({{ m.window_low_offset }} to +{{ m.window_high_offset }} days).
